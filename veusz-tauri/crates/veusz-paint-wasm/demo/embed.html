An embedded Veusz figure

The plot below is a real Veusz document rendered in your browser: its vector scene is drawn by Veusz's Vello painter compiled to WebAssembly, on the GPU via WebGPU. No Python, no Qt, no server in the paint path — just a .scene.json blob and a .wasm module.

Source: examples/fit.vsz · scene captured via capture_document_scene · renderer: veusz-paint-wasm.
