Veusz interactive embed

A live Veusz figure running entirely in your browser (Pyodide + Vello/WebGPU). Drag a rectangle to zoom, shift-drag to pan, hover for data values, double-click to reset, and Edit to change settings. Requires WebGPU (Chrome or Safari 26+) and the headless veusz wheel (built in W5; set via the veusz-wheel attribute or a CDN default).
